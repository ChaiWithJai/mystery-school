# Piano sample

Salamander Grand Piano by Alexander Holm. Recorded Yamaha C5 grand piano.

Source: https://github.com/Tonejs/audio/tree/master/salamander
File: https://tonejs.github.io/audio/salamander/Ds6.mp3
License: Creative Commons Attribution 3.0
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/

The MP3 file is bundled without modification. Playback may transpose the
D-sharp 6 sample to the requested pitch and shape its release envelope.
The sample is an isolated instrument note, not audio from Runaway.
The upstream license and recording notes are in SOURCE-LICENSE.txt.
