Jai requested 7–12 rapid reward moments in the book world within roughly 90 seconds. The same school world now contains ten glowing book pickups along its central path. Walking collects them with a chime, light burst, short authored prompt and persistent progress. The final explicit action opens the learner's own writing. Pickups record exploration, not comprehension, mastery, or a measured dopamine response.

The classics reflection now closes its loop: “Keep this journey in my world” saves an ideas artifact and places its exact action, observation, interpretation, revised belief and next question into a source-linked world memory. Plans remain plans; reported actions remain unverified reports. Boxing memories continue to work alongside these journeys.
