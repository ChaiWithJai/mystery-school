# Presenter sequence

Open `/` for Runaway. Use `?view=forest` to inspect the school homepage separately.

## Story to tell

"A good teacher can change the course of someone's life. A chatbot is not a
teacher. My name is Jai. I want to build a school on my computer, where I can
make a sandbox around what a student already loves.

"A student who struggles with physics might love boxing. We start with a
movement. We notice what changes. Then we work out the physics together.
I am still their teacher. I listen, advocate for them, and find another way
when the first explanation does not help.

"I want to teach courage, passion, and imagination. AI helps me build the
worlds. The relationship stays human."

Say this over the actual interactions, not over additional instruction screens.
Describe the boxing-to-physics connection as the teaching approach, not a
measured learning outcome. Do not label a student as troubled or infer a need
from their activity logs. Teacher authoring and classroom review are future
product work, not shipped capabilities.

## Live walkthrough

| Time | Action | Point to make |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–7 seconds | Hear the piano, then strike **L** with the falling E6 cue. | Make something before explaining it. |
| 7–12 seconds | Keep the take and go directly **Into the ring**. | Start with what the student loves. |
| 12–52 seconds | In Stick & move, choose **Try this in my mirror**, start the camera, then **40-second practice**. | Legs are wheels. Move before the punch; move again after it. |
| 52–60 seconds | Keep a short observation in **What did you notice?**, then **Keep my observation**. Find that exact memory in the school world. | Experience becomes something you can examine, revise and carry into life. |

Allow up to 90 seconds for spoken narration and camera setup. The transitions are manual. The five foundations are available behind the body-map selector; do not try to teach all five in the 40-second segment.

Before presenting, grant camera access through **Start camera** and check the actual room framing, then stop the mirror and return to `/`. Verify audible piano output on the presentation speakers. Browser QA with a source-video fixture does not establish either physical-camera framing or audible output.

The body-map **Hear cue** control reads authored guidance. **Ask local AI for one cue** uses the separately configured local companion, between attempts. Its failure does not turn an authored cue into an AI response. Keep that distinction when presenting. Never describe the pose trail as measured force, depth, technique mastery or a breathing assessment.

Finish with **Write my story** or **Take this into life**. The saved boxing observation retains its original source artifact and trace; the learner's words are not replaced with an invented success story.
